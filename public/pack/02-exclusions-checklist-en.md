# Out-of-Scope / Exclusions Checklist

Use this list in proposals, SOWs, and kickoff emails. Check every item that is **not** included unless added via a paid change order.

**Project:** [PROJECT_NAME]  
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Content & strategy

- [ ] Additional pages / screens beyond [N]
- [ ] Copywriting / rewriting of client-provided text
- [ ] Brand strategy, naming, or positioning workshops
- [ ] Ongoing content calendars or social posting

## Design & product

- [ ] Extra design concepts beyond [N] directions
- [ ] Custom illustrations / icon sets beyond stock
- [ ] Motion / video / animation
- [ ] Design system documentation beyond agreed pages

## Development & tech

- [ ] Integrations not listed in the SOW (CRM, ERP, custom APIs)
- [ ] CMS training beyond [N] hours
- [ ] Performance / SEO audit beyond basic best practices
- [ ] Accessibility audit (WCAG formal report)
- [ ] Hosting, domain, or third-party tool fees

## AI & tooling (AI-era defaults)

- [ ] Client "AI discount" on the agreed fee
- [ ] Unlimited prompt iteration or model experimentation billed as free
- [ ] Training the client's internal team on AI workflows
- [ ] Ownership transfer of private prompts / fine-tunes beyond deliverables

## Project management

- [ ] More than [N] revision rounds
- [ ] Meetings beyond [N] hours / week
- [ ] Same-day turnaround outside agreed SLAs
- [ ] Parallel workstreams without capacity buffer

## Post-launch

- [ ] Maintenance retainer
- [ ] Bug fixes after the warranty window of [N] days
- [ ] New feature requests after final sign-off

## Notes

Anything checked above requires a written change order with fee and timeline before work starts.
