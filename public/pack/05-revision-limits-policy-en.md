# Revision Limits Policy

**Project:** [PROJECT_NAME]  
**Applies to:** all deliverables in the signed SOW dated [DATE]

## Included revisions

- **[N] rounds** of revisions per milestone (default recommendation: 2).
- A “round” = one consolidated batch of feedback from the client’s primary contact, delivered within [X] business days of review start.
- Revisions cover adjustments within the approved direction (copy tweaks, layout polish, minor feature fixes listed in acceptance criteria).

## Not counted as free revisions

- New features, pages, or flows not in the SOW  
- Reopening a closed milestone after written approval  
- Feedback arriving as continuous drips instead of one batch (may be treated as extra rounds)  
- Changes driven by a new stakeholder who was not in kickoff  

## Process

1. Provider delivers the milestone build / draft.  
2. Client sends one consolidated feedback list by [CHANNEL] within [X] days.  
3. Provider implements the round and shares an updated version.  
4. After included rounds are used, further changes require a change order.

## Escalation

If feedback conflicts with the SOW, the provider will flag it within [Y] business days and propose either a clarification or a change order — no silent unpaid expansion.
